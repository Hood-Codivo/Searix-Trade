# Brand — Searix Trade

_Status: active_

Searix Trade is a calm, evidence-led trading terminal for Solana order-book markets. It makes liquidity, execution quality, and potential market unfairness understandable before a trader commits funds.

## Palette

- Canvas: `#F2F3FA`
- Card surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Elevated surface: `#F6F6FC`
- Primary text: `#15161F`
- Secondary text: `#8B8D9F`
- Indigo accent: `#6D5DF6` (gradient `#5B4FE8` → `#8B7CF7`)
- Bid/success: `#22C55E`
- Ask/risk: `#EF4444`
- Informational blue: `#3B82F6`

Indigo is the product accent, used as a solid color and as the hero-card/splash gradient. Green and red are reserved for market direction and trade semantics.

## Typography

- Plus Jakarta Sans: interface copy, labels, headings, and all numeric display (prices, percentages, quantities, addresses, timestamps) — one rounded typeface throughout, no separate monospace family.

## Voice

Plain, measured, and specific. Prefer “Depth is thin beyond $150” over unexplained jargon. Never present probabilistic detection as certainty.
